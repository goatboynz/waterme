## Ã°Å¸Å¡â‚¬ Installation

Installing WaterMe is simple via the Home Assistant Add-on Store:

1. Copy the following URL:
   `https://github.com/goatboynz/waterme`
2. In your Home Assistant dashboard, navigate to **Settings** > **Add-ons**.
3. Click the **Add-on Store** button in the bottom-right.
4. Click the three dots (top-right menu) and select **Repositories**.
5. Paste the URL into the **Add** field and click **Add**.
6. Close the popup and search for **WaterMe** in the store.
7. Click **WaterMe**, then click **Install**.
8. Enable **Show in sidebar** and click **Start**.
